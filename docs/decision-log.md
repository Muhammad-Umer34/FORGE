# FORGE decision log

Every decision in FORGE, what and why, one row each. This is the **only** place decisions are recorded
(see `README.md` for the rules). Decisions D-001 to D-206 were first kept in section 24 of
`FORGE-DOCUMENTS/FORGE_Clarification_Agent_Plan_v6.md` and were moved here unchanged on 2026-11-08.
New decisions are added at the end, under a dated heading.

Status values: **Implemented** (done and checked), **Approved** (agreed, not built), **Proposed** (waiting for approval), **Rejected**, **Superseded**.
